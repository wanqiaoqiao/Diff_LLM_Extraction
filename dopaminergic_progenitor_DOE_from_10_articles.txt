==================================================
1. Consensus Intermediate Stage Map
==================================================

Stage A. Starting pluripotent state / pre-patterning
Typical time range:
- Day -1 to Day 0

Cell state:
- iPSC / hiPSC / hPSC

Typical basal medium:
- mTeSR1, E8, or pluripotent maintenance medium
- some protocols switch immediately into KSR/DMEM or floor-plate induction medium at confluence

Typical factors:
- Y-27632: 10 uM for plating survival

Interpretation:
- This stage mostly standardizes survival, confluence, and colony state.
- It is not the main lineage-defining stage.

Stage B. Neural induction / exit from pluripotency
Typical time range:
- Day 0 to Day 5-7

Cell state:
- early neuroectoderm / neural stem cell-like / early neural progenitor

Typical basal medium families:
- KSR/DMEM-based induction medium
- DMEM/F12 + Neurobasal combinations with N2/B27

Common factors:
- SB431542: usually 10 uM
- LDN193189: 100-200 nM
- alternative BMP inhibition: dorsomorphin 2-5 uM or Noggin 600 ng/mL

Typical markers:
- SOX1, PAX6, Nestin when neural induction is successful
- OCT4 down

Interpretation:
- Dual-SMAD inhibition is the most conserved early step across the 10-paper set.
- This is the first major gate for success because poor neural induction weakens all downstream ventral midbrain specification.

Stage C. Ventral midbrain / floor plate patterning
Typical time range:
- Starts Day 1-3 and usually runs to Day 9-11 or Day 13-14

Cell state:
- ventral neural progenitor
- ventral midbrain progenitor
- floor plate progenitor
- midbrain floor plate progenitor

This stage splits into two protocol families.

Family 1. Classical WNT/FGF8/SHH family
Typical factors:
- SHH: often 100 ng/mL
- Purmorphamine: 1-2 uM
- or SAG: 1 uM
- FGF8 / FGF8a / FGF8b: usually 100 ng/mL
- CHIR99021: 0.8-3 uM
- CHIR often starts around Day 3-4 and runs to Day 10-13

Family 2. RA-based family
Typical factors:
- all-trans RA: 100-800 nM tested, around 200 nM commonly highlighted
- key determinant is exposure duration, especially a 48-hour pulse
- SHH pathway activation via SAG is combined with RA
- this branch explicitly reduces dependence on CHIR/FGF8 patterning

Typical markers for successful Stage C:
- LMX1A+
- FOXA2+
- OTX2+
- CORIN+
- low PAX6 dorsal contamination
- low NKX2.1 or PHOX2B depending on off-target ventral/hindbrain drift

Interpretation:
- This is the core stage for your dopaminergic progenitor DOE.
- The main variable is not simply “make TH+ neurons later”, but whether you generate a clean LMX1A+/FOXA2+/OTX2+/CORIN+ ventral midbrain floor-plate population here.

Stage D. Dopaminergic progenitor stabilization / expansion
Typical time range:
- Day 9-11 to Day 14-20

Cell state:
- midbrain floor plate progenitor
- dopaminergic progenitor
- neurogenic floor plate progenitor
- NURR1-emerging DA progenitor

Typical factors:
- continued low CHIR in some protocols: 0.8-1 uM or up to 3 uM depending on platform
- continued LDN in some expansion systems
- Y-27632 during passaging/replating
- in expansion protocols, SHH/FGF8 may become less important than maintaining progenitor identity and survival

Typical markers:
- LMX1A, FOXA2, OTX2 remain central
- CORIN or LRTM1 for sorting/enrichment
- NURR1 begins to emerge and may define a particularly transplantable window

Interpretation:
- For a dopaminergic progenitor product, this stage is often the endpoint of greatest practical value.
- Multiple papers imply that Day 11-16 is a particularly important decision window for harvest, sorting, cryobanking, or transplantation.

Stage E. DA neuronal maturation
Typical time range:
- Day 12-14 onward to Day 28-50+

Cell state:
- immature dopamine neuron
- maturing DA neuron
- A9 DA neuron in later protocols

Typical factors:
- Ascorbic acid / AA: usually 200 uM or 0.2 mM
- BDNF: commonly 20 ng/mL
- GDNF: commonly 20 ng/mL
- TGF-beta3: 1-2 ng/mL
- dbcAMP / cAMP: 0.5 mM
- DAPT: 1-10 uM in some protocols

Typical markers:
- NURR1
- TH
- later VMAT2, DAT, GIRK2, PITX3, ALDH1A1

Interpretation:
- This stage confirms that the progenitor stage was correct.
- But for optimizing dopaminergic progenitors, this should be treated mainly as a downstream validation stage, not the primary DOE endpoint.

==================================================
2. Factor and Concentration Ranges by Stage
==================================================

Stage A. Pre-patterning
- Y-27632: 10 uM
Reasoning:
- Seen repeatedly for plating survival and replating stability.
- Low-risk to keep fixed in the baseline.

Stage B. Neural induction / dual-SMAD
- SB431542: around 10 uM
- LDN193189: 100-200 nM
- Dorsomorphin: about 2-5 uM when used instead of LDN
- Noggin: up to about 600 ng/mL in some older/classical protocols
Reasoning:
- This is one of the strongest shared consensus steps.
- Most protocols that robustly reach ventral midbrain lineage start here.

Stage C. Ventralization + midbrain patterning, WNT/FGF8/SHH family
- SHH: about 100 ng/mL
- Purmorphamine: 1-2 uM
- SAG: about 1 uM
- FGF8/FGF8a/FGF8b: about 100 ng/mL
- CHIR99021: about 0.8-3 uM
Reasoning:
- These ranges recur across multiple protocol papers.
- CHIR is the most sensitive variable and likely interacts with medium and cell line.
- 0.8-1 uM appears in more tightly controlled midbrain protocols, while 3 uM appears in several classical floor-plate protocols.

Stage C alternative. RA-based midbrain patterning family
- RA: about 100-800 nM tested
- practical center from this set: around 200 nM
- key timing variable: 1 day vs 2 days vs 4 days exposure
Reasoning:
- The RA paper strongly argues that duration matters more than concentration.
- A 48 h RA pulse plus SHH pathway activation gave robust LMX1A+/FOXA2+/OTX2+ vMB progenitors.
- This family should not be mixed blindly with the CHIR/FGF8 family in one first-pass DOE without a branch structure.

Stage D. Progenitor stabilization / sorting / expansion
- CHIR99021 maintenance: around 0.8-1 uM in some progenitor maintenance settings, up to 3 uM in others
- LDN193189 maintenance during expansion: used in some expansion systems
- Y-27632: 10 uM at passaging/replating
Reasoning:
- This stage is less about strong repatterning and more about preserving a clean progenitor identity.
- CORIN and LRTM1 appear as practical enrichment markers.

Stage E. Neuronal maturation support
- Ascorbic acid: about 200 uM / 0.2 mM
- BDNF: 20 ng/mL
- GDNF: 20 ng/mL
- TGF-beta3: 1-2 ng/mL
- dbcAMP or cAMP: 0.5 mM
- DAPT: 1-10 uM
Reasoning:
- These factors are highly recurrent downstream maturation supports.
- For a progenitor DOE, they should mostly be held fixed.

3. Recommended Working Definition of the Dopaminergic Progenitor Endpoint
==================================================

For this project, I recommend defining the desired endpoint operationally as:
- Day 11-16 ventral midbrain dopaminergic progenitor / floor plate progenitor

Primary positive markers:
- LMX1A
- FOXA2
- OTX2
- CORIN if measurable
- optionally EN1 depending on whether you prefer more caudal vMB identity
- NURR1 can be used as a late progenitor / early neurogenic DA marker

Primary negative / exclusion markers:
- OCT4 residual pluripotent cells
- PAX6 high dorsal neural contamination
- NKX2.1 high ventral forebrain contamination
- PHOX2B hindbrain contamination
- KI67 if overproliferation risk matters in a transplantation-oriented workflow

Why this endpoint:
- It matches the most actionable cell state across the selected papers.
- It is early enough to optimize lineage specification before long maturation confounds the result.
- It is also the stage most relevant for sorting, cryobanking, and transplantation strategies.

4. Core Logic for the DOE Structure
==================================================

The 10 papers do not describe one single protocol family.
They cluster into two main strategy families:

Family A:
- dual-SMAD inhibition + SHH/PM or SAG + CHIR +/- FGF8
- this is the classical floor-plate route

Family B:
- dual-SMAD inhibition + RA pulse + SHH/SAG
- this is the RA-based mesencephalic patterning route

Why this matters:
- If you throw RA and CHIR/FGF8 into one undifferentiated DOE, interpretation becomes muddy.
- These are not just dose tweaks of the same pathway logic. They are partly different patterning paradigms.
- Therefore the first DOE should either:
  1. compare the two families as a categorical branch factor, or
  2. choose one family as baseline and optimize within it.

My recommendation:
- Run a branch-comparison mini DOE first.
- Then optimize the winning branch in a second DOE.

5. Proposed DOE
==================================================

Stage 0. Branch comparison DOE
Objective:
- Decide whether your iPSC line behaves better with the classical WNT/FGF8 route or the RA route for generating clean dopaminergic progenitors.

Branch A baseline:
- Day 0-7: SB431542 10 uM + LDN193189 100-200 nM
- SHH 100 ng/mL + Purmorphamine 1-2 uM or SAG 1 uM
- FGF8 100 ng/mL
- CHIR99021 centered around 1 uM to 3 uM, starting around Day 3-4

Branch B baseline:
- Day 0-4: SB431542 + LDN193189
- RA 200 nM for 48 h
- SAG around 1 uM for ventralization
- no CHIR/FGF8 as the primary mesencephalic specification signal

Primary readout at Day 9-11:
- LMX1A+/FOXA2+/OTX2+ fraction
- PAX6 contamination
- NKX2.1 and PHOX2B contamination
- viability / morphology consistency

Decision rule:
- Pick the branch that gives the cleanest and most reproducible ventral midbrain progenitor identity, not just the highest later TH expression.

6. DOE A: Optimization within the Classical WNT/FGF8/SHH Family
==================================================

Objective:
- Maximize clean ventral midbrain dopaminergic progenitor generation at Day 11-16.

Primary response variables:
- LMX1A+/FOXA2+ percentage
- OTX2+ percentage
- CORIN+ percentage if available
- OCT4 residual percentage
- PAX6, NKX2.1, PHOX2B contamination
- viable yield

Secondary response variables:
- NURR1 at Day 14-16
- later TH confirmation at Day 25-30 only as downstream validation

Screening factors:

Factor A. CHIR99021 concentration
Levels:
- 0.8 uM
- 1.5 uM
- 3.0 uM
Logic:
- This is one of the most variable and important factors across the selected articles.
- Lower values tend to reduce hindbrain drift but may underpattern some lines.
- Higher values can improve midbrain induction but also introduce sensitivity and off-target patterning.

Factor B. CHIR start time
Levels:
- Day 3
- Day 4
- Day 5
Logic:
- Different protocols start CHIR in slightly different windows.
- This likely interacts strongly with the pace of neural induction in each iPSC line.

Factor C. CHIR duration
Levels:
- to Day 10
- to Day 11
- to Day 13
Logic:
- Several papers imply the duration window matters as much as the dose.
- Longer exposure may push caudalization too far in some lines.

Factor D. SHH pathway strength
Levels:
- SHH 100 ng/mL + PM 1 uM
- SHH 100 ng/mL + PM 2 uM
- SAG 1 uM
Logic:
- Papers use both SHH+PM and SAG-based ventralization.
- This is a true pathway-implementation variable, not just a minor tweak.

Factor E. FGF8 inclusion
Levels:
- 0 ng/mL
- 100 ng/mL
Logic:
- FGF8 is common in classical protocols, but not universal in newer alternatives.
- Useful as a yes/no screen if resources allow.

Factor F. Dual-SMAD duration
Levels:
- 5 days
- 7 days
- 9 days
Logic:
- Persistent early SMAD inhibition can influence both efficiency and overconstraint of early patterning.
- This also interacts with the timing of CHIR and SHH signals.

Hold fixed during DOE A:
- SB431542 at 10 uM
- LDN193189 at 100 nM as a practical center
- Y-27632 at 10 uM only for plating/replating
- progenitor harvest/readout at Day 11-16
- maturation factors should not be used as optimization variables in this phase

7. DOE B: Optimization within the RA-based Family
==================================================

Objective:
- Maximize robust generation of LMX1A+/FOXA2+/OTX2+ ventral midbrain progenitors using the RA route.

Primary response variables:
- LMX1A+/FOXA2+/OTX2+ percentage at Day 9-14
- low NKX2.1 and low PHOX2B contamination
- viability and reproducibility across replicates

Screening factors:

Factor A. RA concentration
Levels:
- 100 nM
- 200 nM
- 500 nM
Logic:
- The article set suggests RA is relatively concentration-tolerant compared with CHIR, but this still needs empirical tuning.

Factor B. RA duration
Levels:
- 1 day
- 2 days
- 4 days
Logic:
- This is the most important RA-family variable.
- The literature strongly supports duration as the key determinant of regional identity.

Factor C. SHH pathway strength
Levels:
- SAG 0.5 uM
- SAG 1.0 uM
- SHH-equivalent ventralization arm if you want a cross-check
Logic:
- RA alone is not enough; ventralization still needs to be set.

Factor D. Dual-SMAD intensity
Levels:
- SB431542 10 uM + LDN193189 100 nM
- SB431542 10 uM + LDN193189 200 nM
Logic:
- Keeps the early neural conversion controlled while you test RA timing.

Hold fixed during DOE B:
- no CHIR as primary mesencephalic factor
- no FGF8 in the first-pass RA DOE
- readout at Day 9-14

8. Minimal Practical DOE if You Want One First Campaign Only
==================================================

If you want a compact first campaign, I would do this instead of a huge matrix.

Step 1. Compare two branches:
- Classical branch: CHIR 1 uM, SHH 100 ng/mL, PM 2 uM, FGF8 100 ng/mL
- RA branch: RA 200 nM for 48 h + SAG 1 uM

Step 2. If classical branch wins, optimize:
- CHIR concentration
- CHIR timing
- SHH/PM strength
- FGF8 yes/no

Step 3. If RA branch wins, optimize:
- RA duration
- RA concentration
- SAG strength

This is a much cleaner path than trying to optimize everything at once.

9. What to Measure in Each Run
==================================================

At Day 2-4:
- OCT4 down
- SOX1 / early neural induction markers

At Day 9-11:
- LMX1A
- FOXA2
- OTX2
- CORIN if possible
- PAX6
- NKX2.1
- PHOX2B

At Day 14-16:
- LMX1A/FOXA2/OTX2 still maintained
- CORIN or LRTM1 if you plan sorting
- NURR1 emergence
- KI67 if proliferation risk matters

At Day 25-30 for validation only:
- TH
- VMAT2
- GIRK2
- DAT or PITX3 if needed

Why this measurement scheme:
- It tells you where a run fails.
- It distinguishes failure of neural induction from failure of midbrain patterning from failure of later neuronal maturation.
- It also keeps the DOE aligned to progenitor optimization rather than mature neuron optimization.

11. Logic Behind the Summary
==================================================

Selection logic:
- I prioritized local PMC papers that were clearly protocol-oriented and involved iPSC/hPSC differentiation toward midbrain dopaminergic lineage.
- If a paper ended in mature DA neurons but clearly described a progenitor or floor plate stage, I still used it, because your DOE target is the progenitor stage and these papers often define that stage well.
- I deprioritized broad reviews and papers without actionable stage timing.

Aggregation logic:
- I grouped recurring protocol steps by developmental function rather than by exact reagent recipe.
- This revealed a strong shared scaffold:
  iPSC -> neural induction via dual-SMAD -> ventral midbrain/floor-plate patterning -> DA progenitor stabilization -> neuronal maturation.
- I then separated the literature into a classical CHIR/FGF8/SHH family and an RA-based family because they are mechanistically distinct enough that combining them blindly would make DOE results hard to interpret.

Why the DOE focuses on progenitors instead of TH+ neurons:
- The papers consistently show that correct floor-plate / vMB progenitor identity determines downstream DA neuron quality.
- TH appears later and can be influenced by maturation conditions, so it is not the cleanest first-pass optimization endpoint if your true product is a transplantable or bankable progenitor.

==================================================

1. I first screened local PMC XMLs for dopaminergic and ventral midbrain markers.
2. I filtered for protocol-like iPSC/hPSC differentiation papers rather than general disease papers.
3. I cross-checked selected articles against the local extracted trajectory table to recover explicit day and concentration statements.

5. I separated clearly supported consensus from branch-specific strategies.

Main assumptions:
- Your real optimization target is dopaminergic progenitors, not only final TH+ neurons.
- You want a practical experiment design, so the DOE should reduce confounding rather than maximize the number of variables in one shot.
- Your platform is likely a 2D adherent iPSC workflow, not a bioreactor or organoid-only setup.

Limitations:
- Not all 10 selected papers report all concentrations with the same completeness.
- Some articles focus more on sorting, transplantation, or maturation than on the earliest induction steps.
- The local rule-extracted timeline table is imperfect, so I used it only as a pointer and checked key protocol windows against the source XML text.
- Because you asked not to use the API, this summary is a manual synthesis rather than a fresh per-paper LLM extraction pass.

Final recommendation:
- First decide between the classical CHIR/FGF8/SHH family and the RA/SAG family for your iPSC line.
- Then optimize the winning branch around its true high-sensitivity variables.
- Use Day 9-16 ventral midbrain progenitor identity, not just later TH positivity, as the main screening endpoint.
