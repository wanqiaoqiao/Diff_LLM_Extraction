- This version excludes feeder-cell-based protocols from the recommended workflow.
- In this field, many classic human iPSC-to-T-cell papers rely on OP9, OP9-DLL1, or OP9-DLL4 feeders.
- Therefore this feeder-free summary uses a stricter filter for the final recommendation: only feeder-free-compatible logic is retained in the proposed protocol and DOE, while older feeder-based papers are used only as developmental reference for stage logic.

- Build a practical feeder-free DOE for differentiating iPSC/hPSC into cytotoxic CD8+ T cells.
- Preserve the most actionable intermediate windows: hematopoietic progenitor, T-lineage progenitor, double-positive T-cell stage, and CD8 cytotoxic programming.
- Keep the logic, exclusions, and evidence limitations visible.

==================================================
1. Consensus Intermediate Stage Map for a Feeder-Free-Compatible Workflow
==================================================

Stage A. Starting pluripotent state / pre-induction stabilization
Typical time range:
- Day -1 to Day 0

Cell state:
- iPSC / hiPSC / hPSC / T-iPSC

Typical basal medium:
- mTeSR1, E8, or pluripotent maintenance medium

Common factors:
- Y-27632: 10 uM for plating survival when single-cell handling is used

Interpretation:
- This stage standardizes survival, confluence, and colony quality.
- It remains compatible with feeder-free manufacturing.

Stage B. Feeder-free hematopoietic / hemogenic progenitor induction
Typical time range:
- Day 0 to Day 8-12

Cell state:
- mesoderm / hemogenic endothelial-like intermediates
- CD34+CD43+ hematopoietic progenitors
- CD34+ hemato-endothelial progenitors

Typical basal medium families:
- embryoid body or aggregate-based serum-free hematopoietic induction
- defined mesoderm-to-hematopoietic induction medium on matrix-coated plates
- xeno-free or low-complexity hematopoietic induction systems

Common soluble signal modules used across feeder-free hPSC hematopoietic systems:
- BMP4
- VEGF
- SCF
- FLT3L in some handoff systems
- optional TPO or other HSPC-support supplements depending on platform

Typical markers:
- CD34+
- CD43+
- low OCT4
- CD31 may appear in the hemogenic endothelial window

Interpretation:
- This is the first major gate for success.
- If feeder-free hematopoietic output is weak, no later feeder-free T-cell stage will be reliable.

Stage C. Feeder-free T-lineage commitment under immobilized Notch support
Typical time range:
- Starts around Day 8-12 and often runs to Day 25-35

Cell state:
- T-lineage progenitors
- DN-like T-cell progenitors
- early thymocyte-like cells

Feeder-free-compatible platform concepts:
- immobilized DLL4 or DLL1 protein on coated plates
- artificial thymic support systems without stromal feeder cells
- retronectin plus Notch-ligand presentation systems
- biomaterial or organoid-inspired thymic niche mimics without live feeder cells

Common factors:
- IL-7
- FLT3L
- SCF

Typical markers:
- CD5+
- CD7+
- declining CD34
- early T-lineage progression markers depending on platform

Interpretation:
- This is the core stage for your feeder-free CD8 T-cell DOE.
- The main question is whether your Notch presentation is strong and durable enough to replace feeder-cell instruction.

Stage D. Double-positive immature T-cell stage
Typical time range:
- Day 28-40

Cell state:
- CD4+CD8+ double-positive immature T cells
- pre-selection / immature TCR-positive T cells depending on the system

Feeder-free-compatible handling:
- continued immobilized DLL4 or artificial thymic support until robust DP emergence
- transfer to fresh coated Notch surfaces at fixed intervals instead of feeder passage

Typical markers:
- CD4+CD8+
- CD5+
- CD3 variable
- TCRalpha beta emerging depending on the exact system

Interpretation:
- This remains the most actionable developmental checkpoint before attempting to generate functional cytotoxic CD8 single-positive cells.
- In feeder-free systems, failure here usually means Notch presentation or cytokine timing is insufficient.

Stage E. Feeder-free CD8 single-positive specification and cytotoxic programming
Typical time range:
- Day 40 to Day 55-65

Cell state:
- CD4-negative CD8-positive T cells
- CD8alpha beta SP T cells
- antigen-specific CTLs or engineered cytotoxic CD8 products

Feeder-free-compatible maturation options:
- anti-CD3/CD28 bead or plate-based stimulation
- peptide-pulsed non-feeder APC substitutes or defined antigen-presentation systems
- IL-7 support
- IL-21 support
- optional IL-15 depending on product goals, though this must be watched to avoid NK-like drift

Typical markers:
- CD4-negative CD8-positive
- CD8alpha beta+
- CD3+
- TCRalpha beta+
- antigen tetramer positivity in antigen-specific systems
- IFNg production
- target-cell killing

Interpretation:
- This is the stage where feeder-free developmental success is converted into usable cytotoxic function.
- It should still be treated as a downstream optimization stage after the developmental checkpoints are working.

Stage F. Expanded feeder-free cytotoxic CD8 product
Typical time range:
- Day 55-75+

Cell state:
- expanded cytotoxic CD8 product
- antigen-specific CTLs or engineered CD8alpha beta T-cell products

Common readouts:
- IFNg
- IL-2
- degranulation / killing assays
- tumor-cell killing in vitro
- in vivo activity in translational settings when relevant

Interpretation:
- This stage validates that the feeder-free developmental backbone is correct.
- But for DOE purposes, Stages B-E are still the main optimization targets.

2. Factor and Concentration Ranges by Stage
==================================================

Stage A. Pre-induction stabilization
- Y-27632: 10 uM
Reasoning:
- Recurrent survival aid for plating and replating.
- Fully compatible with feeder-free workflows.

Stage B. Feeder-free hematopoietic induction
Practical feeder-free range set for DOE:
- BMP4: 10-30 ng/mL
- VEGF: 10-40 ng/mL
- SCF: 20-50 ng/mL
- optional FLT3L handoff support: 5-10 ng/mL
Reasoning:
- Exact T-cell-specific feeder-free hematopoietic induction recipes vary across platforms.
- These ranges are anchored in common hPSC hematopoietic induction logic and are narrow enough to be experimentally useful.
- The key goal is to generate a clean CD34+CD43+ intermediate, not to optimize every HSPC cytokine simultaneously.

Stage C-D. Feeder-free T-lineage commitment / DP generation
- IL-7: about 2.5-10 ng/mL, practical center 5 ng/mL
- FLT3L: about 2.5-10 ng/mL, practical center 5 ng/mL
- SCF: about 2.5-10 ng/mL, practical center 5 ng/mL
- DLL4 or DLL1 surface density: must be empirically optimized per coating system
Reasoning:
- The IL-7 / FLT3L / SCF trio remains the strongest shared T-lineage cytokine logic even when feeders are removed.
- In feeder-free systems, immobilized Notch signal strength becomes a first-order process variable.

Stage E. Feeder-free CD8 SP induction and cytotoxic programming
- IL-7: about 5 ng/mL
- IL-21: about 10 ng/mL, testable range 0-20 ng/mL
- anti-CD3/CD28 stimulation intensity: must be titrated by bead-to-cell ratio or coating density
Reasoning:
- IL-21 remains one of the most recurrent late-stage CTL-support cytokines.
- In feeder-free systems, the stimulation format replaces much of the biological variability that feeder APC-like systems used to introduce.

Stage F. Late expansion / functional validation
- repeat stimulation interval often 5-9 days depending on the system
Reasoning:
- Over-stimulation can drive dysfunction or exhaustion.
- Expansion variables should be optimized only after the developmental backbone is stable.

Explicit exclusions from this version:
- OP9
- OP9-DLL1
- OP9-DLL4
- stromal feeder passage
- live feeder APC expansion platforms
Reasoning:
- These violate the feeder-free requirement.

3. Recommended Working Definition of the Intermediate and Final Endpoints
==================================================

For this feeder-free project, I recommend using three operational endpoints.

Primary optimization endpoint 1:
- Day 8-12 hematopoietic progenitor checkpoint

Primary positive markers:
- CD34
- CD43
- low OCT4

Primary optimization endpoint 2:
- Day 28-40 DP immature T-cell checkpoint

Primary positive markers:
- CD4+CD8+
- CD5+
- CD7+
- emerging CD3 / TCR if measurable

Primary optimization endpoint 3:
- Day 45-65 CD8 cytotoxic differentiation checkpoint

Primary positive markers:
- CD4-negative CD8-positive
- CD8alpha beta
- CD3
- TCRalpha beta
- antigen tetramer positive if applicable
- IFNg response
- target-cell killing

Primary negative / exclusion markers:
- residual OCT4
- low CD34/CD43 emergence
- failure to generate DP cells
- high NK-like contamination if checked
- poor expansion after CD8 induction

Why this endpoint structure:
- Feeder-free systems are most likely to fail at the Notch-dependent developmental stages.
- Measuring only final cytotoxicity is too noisy to diagnose that problem.
- These three checkpoints make the bottleneck visible.

4. Core Logic for the Feeder-Free DOE Structure
==================================================

The feeder-free literature is less mature than the classical feeder-based literature.
Therefore the DOE should be built around the variables that most plausibly replace feeder-cell instruction.

Feeder-free priority variables:
- hematopoietic induction quality
- immobilized Notch ligand implementation
- IL-7 / FLT3L / SCF support during T-lineage progression
- timing of transfer from hematopoietic stage to Notch stage
- anti-CD3/CD28 or defined antigen-presentation intensity during CD8 programming

Why this matters:
- In feeder-based protocols, OP9 and OP9-DLL1/DLL4 silently provide complex adhesive and signaling cues.
- Once feeders are removed, Notch presentation format and timing become much more important.
- Therefore a feeder-free DOE should focus more on coated-ligand presentation and stage timing than on copying every feeder-era step literally.

My recommendation:
- Use a feeder-free hematopoietic induction stage.
- Then transfer to an immobilized DLL4-centered T-lineage stage.
- Only after a reliable DP stage is obtained should you optimize CD8 cytotoxic programming.

5. Proposed Feeder-Free DOE Framework
==================================================

Stage 0. Route-selection mini DOE
Objective:
- Decide whether your cells behave best with DLL4-coated, DLL1-coated, or alternative artificial thymic feeder-free presentation.

Route A baseline:
- feeder-free hematopoietic induction to Day 10
- transfer to DLL4-coated feeder-free T-lineage medium with IL-7 5 ng/mL + FLT3L 5 ng/mL + SCF 5 ng/mL
- fixed coating refresh schedule
- assess DP around Day 30-35
- induce CD8 cytotoxic programming with anti-CD3/CD28 plus IL-7 5 ng/mL + IL-21 10 ng/mL

Route B baseline:
- same as Route A but DLL1-coated instead of DLL4-coated

Route C baseline:
- alternative artificial thymic support surface benchmark arm if available

Primary readout at Day 30-35:
- CD4+CD8+ DP percentage
- CD5 positivity
- CD7 positivity
- CD3/TCR emergence
- viability / expansion consistency

Decision rule:
- Pick the route that gives the cleanest and most reproducible DP and CD8 SP generation, not merely the highest endpoint killing after repeated stimulation.

6. DOE A: Optimization within the Feeder-Free Developmental Backbone
==================================================

Objective:
- Maximize clean feeder-free CD8 T-lineage commitment with good DP yield and efficient later conversion into cytotoxic CD8 SP cells.

Primary response variables:
- Day 8-12 CD34+ and CD43+ percentage
- Day 28-40 CD4+CD8+ DP percentage
- Day 28-40 CD5+ percentage
- Day 45-65 CD4-negative CD8-positive percentage
- viable yield
- expansion consistency

Secondary response variables:
- CD3 and tetramer signal
- IFNg and killing only as downstream validation

Screening factors:

Factor A. Hematopoietic induction duration
Levels:
- 8 days
- 10 days
- 12 days
Logic:
- The feeder-free handoff into Notch support is likely timing-sensitive.
- Too early may underdeliver progenitors; too late may reduce T-lineage competency.

Factor B. DLL4 / DLL1 implementation
Levels:
- DLL4-coated
- DLL1-coated
- mixed DLL4/DLL1 or alternative artificial thymic support arm
Logic:
- This is the most important feeder-replacement variable.
- DLL4 is often the most biologically plausible first choice for stronger T-lineage commitment.

Factor C. IL-7 concentration
Levels:
- 2.5 ng/mL
- 5 ng/mL
- 10 ng/mL
Logic:
- 5 ng/mL is the practical center from the feeder-era literature and still a strong feeder-free starting point.

Factor D. FLT3L concentration
Levels:
- 2.5 ng/mL
- 5 ng/mL
- 10 ng/mL
Logic:
- FLT3L supports early progenitor fitness and T-lineage progression in both feeder and feeder-free logic.

Factor E. SCF concentration
Levels:
- 2.5 ng/mL
- 5 ng/mL
- 10 ng/mL
Logic:
- SCF remains part of the core T-lineage support trio.

Factor F. Notch-surface refresh interval
Levels:
- every 4 days
- every 6 days
- every 8 days
Logic:
- In feeder-free systems, loss of ligand presentation over time may become a hidden bottleneck.

Hold fixed during DOE A:
- Y-27632 only for setup
- one iPSC/T-iPSC clone at a time
- one matrix/coating chemistry platform
- no aggressive late antigen restimulation until developmental readouts are collected

7. DOE B: Feeder-Free CD8 Cytotoxic Programming Optimization
==================================================

Objective:
- Starting from the best feeder-free developmental backbone, maximize generation of functional cytotoxic CD8 SP cells.

Primary response variables:
- CD4-negative CD8-positive percentage
- CD8alpha beta expression
- CD3 intensity
- tetramer positivity if antigen-specific
- IFNg production
- specific target-cell killing

Screening factors:

Factor A. Stimulation format
Levels:
- anti-CD3/CD28 only
- defined peptide-presentation only
- anti-CD3/CD28 followed by defined peptide-presentation
Logic:
- This replaces the feeder-era LCL-driven variability with defined stimulation formats.

Factor B. IL-21 concentration
Levels:
- 0 ng/mL
- 10 ng/mL
- 20 ng/mL
Logic:
- 10 ng/mL remains the literature center.
- This is a high-signal late-stage variable for CTL function.

Factor C. IL-7 support during CD8 induction
Levels:
- 0 ng/mL
- 5 ng/mL
- 10 ng/mL
Logic:
- May support survival through the DP-to-CD8 transition.

Factor D. Restimulation interval
Levels:
- every 5 days
- every 7 days
- every 9 days
Logic:
- Over-restimulation can impair product quality even in feeder-free systems.

Hold fixed during DOE B:
- use the best DOE A feeder-free developmental condition
- do not reopen early hematopoietic or Notch-surface variables

8. Minimal Practical Feeder-Free DOE if You Want One First Campaign Only
==================================================

If you want one compact feeder-free campaign instead of two sequential campaigns, I recommend a reduced DOE around the DLL4-coated developmental route.

Recommended reduced factor set:
- hematopoietic handoff day: Day 8 / Day 10 / Day 12
- Notch ligand arm: DLL4 vs DLL1
- IL-7: 2.5 / 5 / 10 ng/mL
- IL-21 during CD8 induction: 0 / 10 / 20 ng/mL
- stimulation format: anti-CD3/CD28 only vs defined peptide presentation vs sequential anti-CD3/CD28 then peptide presentation

Keep fixed:
- SCF 5 ng/mL
- FLT3L 5 ng/mL
- one coating chemistry
- one clone and one antigen system

Primary readout:
- Day 8-12: CD34, CD43, OCT4
- Day 28-40: CD4, CD8, CD5, CD7, CD3
- Day 45-65: CD8alpha beta, CD3, tetramer, IFNg, killing assay

Why this compact feeder-free DOE:
- It keeps the highest-signal controllable variables.
- It focuses on the true feeder replacement problem: Notch presentation and developmental timing.
- It tells you whether the main bottleneck is hematopoietic output, DP formation, or CD8 cytotoxic programming.

9. Practical Feeder-Free Anchor Protocol to Start From
==================================================

Recommended anchor protocol:
- Day -1 to 0: seed healthy iPSC/T-iPSC with Y-27632 10 uM if single-cell handling is used
- Day 0-10: feeder-free hematopoietic induction in a defined mesoderm/hemato-endothelial system using BMP4 20 ng/mL + VEGF 20 ng/mL + SCF 40 ng/mL as a practical starting center
- Day 10: transfer CD34+/CD43+-enriched cells to DLL4-coated feeder-free T-lineage medium with IL-7 5 ng/mL + FLT3L 5 ng/mL + SCF 5 ng/mL
- Day 10-30+: maintain on refreshed DLL4-coated surfaces at a fixed interval and monitor CD5/CD7 progression and DP emergence
- Day 30-40: assess CD4+CD8+ DP cells with CD5, CD7, and CD3
- Day 40 onward: induce CD8 SP maturation with anti-CD3/CD28-based stimulation and/or defined peptide presentation using IL-7 5 ng/mL + IL-21 10 ng/mL
- Day 45-65: assess CD4-negative CD8-positive, CD8alpha beta, CD3, tetramer, IFNg, and target-cell killing

Why this feeder-free anchor protocol:
- It follows the same developmental logic as the classic CTL regeneration literature while removing feeder cells.
- It makes the feeder-replacement variables explicit and testable.
- It is easier to troubleshoot systematically than mixing feeder-free and feeder-based steps.

==================================================
10. Logic, Evidence, and Self-Check
==================================================

Main synthesis logic:
- The strongest developmental consensus across the articles is still:
  1. stabilize pluripotent cells,
  2. induce a hematopoietic progenitor state,
  3. commit into Notch-dependent T-lineage progenitors,
  4. generate DP immature T cells,
  5. only then drive CD8 SP cytotoxic programming and expansion.
- Because feeder cells are excluded, the most important replacement variables become hematopoietic handoff timing, immobilized Notch presentation, and defined stimulation format.
- The most reproducible soluble-factor logic remains IL-7 / FLT3L / SCF for T-lineage progression and IL-21 for late CTL support.

Why I prioritize Stages B-D in the feeder-free DOE:
- Feeder-free systems are most likely to fail before the final cytotoxic stage.
- Day 8-12 and Day 28-40 checkpoints are therefore more diagnostic than endpoint killing alone.

Why I excluded feeder cells completely from the recommended protocol:
- The user requirement is strict feeder-free.
- Therefore OP9, OP9-DLL1, OP9-DLL4, and live feeder APC-style systems were excluded from the proposed workflow even though they are historically important.

How I used feeder-era papers anyway:
- I used them to infer which intermediate stages and markers matter most.
- I did not use them as the recommended operating conditions.
- This is an inference step from the literature, not a claim that feeder-free and feeder-based systems are equivalent.

Main evidence threads behind this summary:
- Classical T-iPSC regeneration papers define the developmental stage order and the importance of the DP window.
- Multiple studies converge on IL-7, FLT3L, and SCF as the core T-lineage support trio.
- Late-stage CTL generation repeatedly uses IL-21 plus defined activation formats.
- Translational feeder-free T-cell work suggests immobilized DLL4 or artificial thymic support is the most rational replacement for stromal feeder Notch signaling.

Self-check and limitations:
- The feeder-free human iPSC-to-cytotoxic-CD8 literature is still thinner than the feeder-based literature.
- Some selected papers are protocol-informing rather than fully feeder-free end-to-end demonstrations.
- Exact DLL4 coating density, surface chemistry, and artificial thymic support format are still likely to be lab-specific.
- I therefore treated this as a feeder-free developmental blueprint and DOE rather than a claim of one universally validated manufacturing protocol.
- The most likely first source of variability will be the efficiency of feeder-free Notch presentation and the quality of the Day 8-12 hematopoietic intermediate.

Bottom-line recommendation:
- Start with a fully feeder-free hematopoietic induction stage.
- Use DLL4-coated feeder-free T-lineage commitment as the default developmental backbone.
- Optimize the developmental backbone first, then optimize feeder-free CD8 cytotoxic programming.
